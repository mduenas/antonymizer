Antonymizer is a timed antonym game. A word appears, and you and a computer opponent race to tap the opposite word.

A match is five rounds. Each round is ten questions, with five seconds on the clock. The player who wins more rounds wins the match. A round or a match can tie.

Pick an opponent before you play:

• Rookie Bot (Easy) — a beginner opponent, good for practice
• Challenger Bot (Medium) — a balanced opponent and a fair challenge
• Expert Bot (Hard) — a skilled opponent, not easy to beat
• Master Bot (Expert) — the ultimate challenge

A correct answer scores 100 points, plus up to 50 more when you answer quickly. A streak of correct answers multiplies that score, up to 3×. Wrong answers and timeouts score nothing.

Points stay on this device and set your rank:

• Bronze — start
• Silver — 1,000 points
• Gold — 3,000
• Platinum — 6,000
• Diamond — 10,000
• Master — 15,000
• Grandmaster — 25,000

The home screen shows your rank, total points, games, wins, win rate, and best streak. The leaderboard is a history of your own matches on this phone, and you can filter it by difficulty. There is no account, no sign-in, and no online multiplayer.

More than 900 antonym pairs ship with the app, from short easy words to harder vocabulary.

You can use up to two hints in a match. Each hint plays a rewarded ad, then removes two wrong answers.

An interstitial ad may play when a match ends, and not more often than once every three minutes. The one-time Remove Ads purchase turns those interstitial ads off. Rewarded ads can still be offered.

Choose a display name in Settings. It stays on the device. Settings can also clear your saved stats, leaderboard, and name.
